# Comparable Companies Analysis | Indian FMCG Sector

## Project Overview

This project presents a Comparable Companies Analysis (CCA) of **Colgate-Palmolive (India) Ltd.** against selected publicly listed companies in the Indian FMCG sector.

The objective is to compare financial performance, profitability, growth, and market valuation using publicly available financial information.

**Financial Data Collection Date:** 28 October 2026

## Companies Analysed

- Colgate-Palmolive (India) Ltd. — Target Company
- Hindustan Unilever Ltd.
- Godrej Consumer Products Ltd.
- Dabur India Ltd.
- Marico Ltd.

## Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel:** Financial calculations, comparable company analysis, valuation multiples, and data visualisation

- **Microsoft Word:** Detailed documentation

## Methodology

1. Selected the target company and relevant industry peers.
2. Collected publicly available financial information.
3. Standardised financial metrics for comparison.
4. Calculated enterprise value and equity value.
5. Analysed valuation multiples, including EV/EBITDA, EV/Revenue, and P/E.
6. Compared financial growth and profitability across companies.
7. Interpreted the results and documented the findings.

## Key Financial Metrics

- Market Capitalisation
- Enterprise Value
- Revenue
- EBITDA and EBITDA Margin
- Earnings per Share (EPS)
- Revenue Growth
- P/E Ratio
- EV/EBITDA Multiple
- EV/Revenue Multiple

## Key Findings — Colgate-Palmolive (India) Ltd.

### 1. Profitability
- **FY2027E EBITDA Margin:** 29.53%, the highest among the selected peers.
- **EBITDA Growth:** 4.60%, increasing from ₹1,870 Cr. to ₹1,956 Cr.
- **Margin Pressure:** EBITDA margin is projected to decline from 30.99% to 29.53%.

### 2. Revenue Growth
- **Projected Revenue Growth:** 9.76%, from ₹6,035 Cr. to ₹6,624 Cr.
- **Peer Comparison:** Colgate's growth exceeds HUL and Dabur but trails Godrej Consumer.
- **Key Observation:** Moderate revenue growth combined with relatively strong operating profitability.

### 3. Relative Valuation
| Valuation Method | Implied Share Price | Upside/Downside |
|---|---:|---:|
| EV/Revenue | ₹1,442.39 | -10.02% |
| EV/EBITDA | ₹1,894.24 | +18.17% |
| P/E | ₹1,894.65 | +18.18% |

**Current Market Price:** ₹1,603.00

**Key Observation:** Colgate trades below the selected peer median on forward EV/EBITDA, while the three valuation methods produce different implied share prices.

### 4. Overall Conclusion
Colgate-Palmolive demonstrates **strong operating profitability, steady projected revenue growth, and mixed relative valuation signals**.

The EV/EBITDA and P/E methods indicate approximately **18% potential upside**, while EV/Revenue suggests approximately **10% downside**.

These findings highlight the importance of evaluating multiple valuation methodologies alongside financial performance and peer comparisons.
